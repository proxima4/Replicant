REPLICANT - installing in FL Studio (Windows)
=============================================

1. Copy the whole "Replicant.vst3" folder into:
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
   (Windows will ask for administrator permission - click Continue.)

2. In FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins.
   Tick the star next to "Replicant" so it shows in your plugin lists.

3. Add it to a project: Channel rack > + > Replicant.

"Replicant.exe" is a standalone version you can run without FL Studio
(choose your audio and MIDI devices with the Options button).

Sounds
------
* The coloured buttons above the ribbon are the CS-80's 22 preset voices.
* FILM SCORE SOUNDS opens 20 more in the style of its famous soundtracks.
* Click the green ESPER screen for the full list, or use < >.
  All 42 also appear in FL Studio's own preset menu for the plugin.
* MEMORY 1-4: press STORE, then a number, to keep the current panel.
  Press a number to recall it. Memories are saved with your FL project.
* SAVE / LOAD store sound files (.rpl) in Documents\Replicant Sounds.

Playing it like a CS-80
-----------------------
* Aftertouch is half the instrument. Most sounds respond to it with
  brightness, swell or vibrato, and polyphonic aftertouch works per note.
  No aftertouch on your keyboard? SETUP > MOD WHEEL > PRESSURE turns the
  mod wheel into aftertouch.
* RIBBON (the strip above the keys): click, then slide left or right to
  bend. It springs back when you let go. SETUP sets its range.
* MIDI it responds to: notes, velocity, polyphonic and channel
  aftertouch, pitch wheel, mod wheel (CC1), sustain (CC64), and MPE
  (switch it on in SETUP).

Quick tour
----------
CHANNEL I / II  Two complete synths per voice: FEET, VCO (saw/square,
                PW, PWM, noise), VCF (high-pass then low-pass), the
                IL-AL-A-D-R filter envelope, VCA with sine, touch response.
SUB OSC         The LFO, to VCO / VCF / VCA.
RING MOD        Ring modulator with its own attack/decay sweep.
TOUCH           Initial pitch bend, and aftertouch to pitch/vibrato/speed.
OVERALL         Global brilliance and resonance.
KEYBOARD        Brightness and level scaling for the low / high keys.
DELAY / HALL    Echo and a big hall reverb.
AGE             How "analog" the voices are: tolerances and drift.

Hover over any control to see its exact value on the ESPER screen.
Double-click a control to reset it. Drag the window corner to resize.
